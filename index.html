01 / Purpose

Technology that supports
the people on the ground.

The programme focuses on assistance, rescue and accountable information support. Human responders remain responsible for decisions that affect people.

01 — RESCUE

Reach difficult places

Explore teleoperated platforms for disaster assessment, search and rescue, and safer access to hazardous environments.

02 — AWARENESS

Improve visibility

Evaluate camera, lighting and thermal sensors to help trained teams understand environmental conditions.

03 — ACCOUNTABILITY

Support human judgment

Organize authorized incident notes and evidence records with clear provenance and human review.

02 / Research areas

Designed around
practical response needs.

Each capability remains a proposed research area until independently tested, approved and supported by documented evidence.

SEARCH & RESCUE

Thermal-assisted search

Assess heat-sensing for low-visibility rescue and hazard awareness. Thermal readings are not proof of identity or wrongdoing.

VICTIM SUPPORT

Human-trafficking response

Explore victim-centred rescue support, discreet help requests, interpreter access and secure referrals led by specialist responders.

SCENE SUPPORT

AI-assisted documentation

Evaluate human-reviewed transcription, translation and incident timelines. Original evidence remains authoritative.

INSPECTION SUPPORT

Authorized inspections

Research sensor alerts that may assist a specific lawful inspection by trained personnel. An alert is never proof.

DISASTER RESPONSE

Hazard assessment

Explore remote observation in fire, flood, storm and structural-collapse scenarios before responders enter.

HUMAN CONTROL

Remote supervision

Prioritize operator control, clear status, safe-stop behavior, secure communications and documented mission boundaries.

04 / Safety by design

Human authority is
not optional.

Robotics can support responders; it must not become an automated enforcement authority. Safety, privacy and public accountability are design requirements.

No autonomous arrests or force

No autonomous pursuit, apprehension, restraint, coercive crowd control or physical intervention.

No weapons or electric nets

No weapons, electrified nets, projectiles or payloads intended to shock, entangle or incapacitate people.

No identity or status profiling

No facial recognition, voiceprint identification, immigration-status profiling or AI criminality scoring.

Privacy and evidence safeguards

Audio capture off by default; lawful purpose, restricted access, original evidence preservation and human review.
